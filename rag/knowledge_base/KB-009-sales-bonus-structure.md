# Sales rep bonus structure — FY 2026

This excerpt of the HR handbook covers commission rules for the
inside sales team only. Outside brokers are paid per the broker
contract (KB-011 + the individual master service agreement on file
with HR).

## Base

Inside reps draw a fixed monthly salary plus quarterly variable.
Variable is paid only on **collected revenue**, not booked revenue.
Late or written-off invoices reduce or eliminate the variable.

## Quarterly tiers

| Quarter target (₹ collected, lakhs) | Variable as % of base salary |
| --- | --- |
| < 25 | 0 |
| 25 – 49 | 25% |
| 50 – 99 | 60% |
| 100 – 149 | 100% |
| 150+ | 150% (capped) |

Targets are set per rep per region by the Sales Head in week 1 of
each quarter and locked in writing. No mid-quarter target moves.

## Multipliers

- **New-customer bonus** — flat ₹25 000 per new export buyer that
  completes their first 3 orders inside the quarter. Capped at 5
  per rep per quarter.
- **Memo-conversion multiplier** — 1.10× the variable for the
  quarter if the rep's memo-to-sale conversion rate stays above
  60% across the quarter.

## Clawbacks

- Any sale returned within 7 days (KB-006) → variable from that
  sale is reversed.
- Any sale that books but isn't collected within 90 DPD → variable
  is reduced pro-rata against the unpaid balance.
- Any thread the rep failed to log to Mahant Threads within the
  KB-008 window for more than 5 incidents in a quarter → 10%
  variable reduction.

## Tax + payout

Variable is paid with the Q-end payslip after TDS. Net amounts are
visible to each rep on the HR Portal under My Compensation > FY26
Variable.

For appeals, file a written request with HR within 30 days of payout.
