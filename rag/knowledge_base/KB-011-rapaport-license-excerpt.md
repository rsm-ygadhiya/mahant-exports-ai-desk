# Rapaport price list — license terms (excerpt)

Pramukh Gems holds an annual Rapaport Premium License (license
number `RAP-IND-2026-00417`). The license covers internal use by
Pramukh's sales desk and our automated pricing tooling. It does **not**
cover redistribution to customers, brokers, or any third party.

This excerpt reproduces the four clauses that affect day-to-day
desk operations. The full agreement is on file with Legal — request
via the HR portal under `Contracts > Vendor Agreements`.

## §3.2 — Internal use only

> The Licensee may use the weekly Rapaport Price List and the
> Rapaport Price Sheet ("the Materials") solely for the internal
> business operations of the Licensee, including the pricing of
> inventory, the generation of internal quotations, and the
> training of employees.

In practice: the `rap_weekly_seed.csv` table is internal data. It
must not be exported, screenshotted, or pasted into any external
communication.

## §3.4 — Customer-facing quotations

> Quotations issued to customers must be expressed in the
> Licensee's own pricing terms. The Licensee shall not disclose
> the underlying Rapaport rate or the percentage discount from
> Rapaport to any customer.

In practice: WhatsApp negotiations and storefront listings show
only **our** price. Never quote "10% off Rap" — quote `$X/ct`
directly.

## §5.1 — Audit right

> The Licensor reserves the right to audit the Licensee's use of
> the Materials no more than twice per calendar year on 30 days'
> written notice.

A Rapaport audit was last conducted on 2025-09-12. No findings.
Our internal compliance owner (Sales Head) is the named point of
contact.

## §7 — Renewal

The current license runs from **2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31** and
auto-renews unless either party gives 60 days' written notice. The
renewal price is anchored to the Rapaport published Tier-1 dealer
rate and reviewed each November.

## What to do if a customer asks about Rap pricing

Quote our price. If pressed: *"Our pricing reflects Rap movements
plus our own factors including market depth, age, and demand. We
don't share the raw Rap value publicly."* (See KB-008 for the
broader negotiation guidance.)
