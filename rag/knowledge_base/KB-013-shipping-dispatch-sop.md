# Shipping and dispatch SOP

Covers domestic dispatch within India and export dispatch to USA,
Dubai, Hong Kong, and EU. Anything else routes through Operations
manually (see KB-014 if it's contested).

## Dispatch readiness checklist

A stone is **dispatch-ready** when:

1. `Diamond.status == Sold` AND `Payment.status in {Paid, Advance}`.
2. The buyer's KYC + AML clearance is current (KB-002).
3. The original IGI certificate is in the dispatch envelope.
4. Vault has done the **outbound weigh-in** and the weight matches
   the certificate (KB-005 intake rule, applied in reverse).
5. The packed weight slip is signed by two ops staff.

Stones for **memo** dispatch follow the same checklist except the
payment gate is replaced by a **signed memo confirmation** from the
customer (the standard PDF generated by Mahant on memo approval).

## Couriers

| Lane | Primary courier | Backup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic (within Maharashtra)   | Sequel Logistics       | Brink's |
| Domestic (other India)          | Brink's                | Malca-Amit |
| USA / Dubai / HK                | Malca-Amit             | Brink's |
| EU                              | Brink's                | Malca-Amit |
| EU < ₹1 L value                 | FedEx Priority Plus    | DHL Express |

Use of any courier outside this list requires written Owner approval
and an insurance pre-clear from our broker (Howden India).

## Export documentation

Each export dispatch needs:

- Commercial invoice (auto-generated by Mahant on `/admin/sales/<id>/invoice`)
- Packing list (auto)
- Kimberley Process Certificate (manually fetched from the GJEPC portal)
- Form GR (RBI / FEMA — finance owns this)
- Airway bill (the courier issues this)

All five PDFs go into `data/exports/<order_id>/` and the Mahant
`Order.dispatched_at` timestamp is set the moment the airway bill
number is logged.

## Re-dispatch (after return)

If a stone returns and goes back out (KB-006), the new dispatch
gets a **new** invoice number and a fresh export documentation set.
Re-using the old invoice has caused two GST notices in the last
two years — Finance: never do this.

## Time targets

- Dispatch-ready → courier hand-off: **same business day** for any
  order received before 14:00 IST.
- Hand-off → tracking update: **within 4 hours**, customer notified
  via WhatsApp + email.
- Domestic delivery SLA: **2 working days** Maharashtra, **4** rest of India.
- Export delivery SLA: **5 working days** USA/EU, **3** Dubai/HK.

Misses go to Operations weekly review.
