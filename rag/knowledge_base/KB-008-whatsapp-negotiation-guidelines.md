# WhatsApp negotiation guidelines

Most of our negotiations still happen on WhatsApp. This is the
playbook every rep follows. Following it is non-optional — these
rules exist because we lost three deals in 2025 to inconsistent
behaviour and one regulatory near-miss to off-the-record pricing.

## Always

1. **Log every offer into Mahant Threads within 30 minutes.** No
   exceptions. The `negotiation-threads` skill in the admin app
   reads these notes; missing notes show up as "stale thread"
   warnings on the morning briefing.
2. **Quote the listed price first**, even if you expect a counter.
   Letting the customer counter sets the anchor at your favour.
3. **Reference the certificate**, never the photo. "The IGI report
   shows VS1, eye-clean, no fluorescence" carries weight; "looks
   really clean" doesn't.
4. **Confirm payment terms in writing** before any price commitment.
   "Net 60" said verbally is worth nothing if there's no paper trail.

## Never

1. **Never quote below the margin floor** stated in
   `Diamond.minPrice`. The floor is set by the Owner and is
   non-negotiable. If a counter requires going below, escalate.
2. **Never agree to a memo (consignment) verbally.** Memos go
   through the admin app so they create an audit trail (and so the
   stone's status updates to `OnMemo` and the rest of the desk
   doesn't try to sell it twice — see KB-001).
3. **Never share a Rapaport price sheet image.** Our licence is
   private (see KB-011). Quote in our terms, not Rap's.
4. **Never discuss other customers by name.** "Another buyer is
   also interested" is fine. "CUST0042 also wanted this stone" is a
   confidentiality breach.

## Tone

We're a 30-year-old family business in Surat doing six-figure-USD
deals. Be warm, but commercial. No emojis except 💎 and 🙏 (sparingly).
No slang. Reply within 4 working hours during the IST day; same-day
end-of-day during your counterparty's timezone if export.

## When to escalate

- Customer asks for terms outside KB-003
- Customer disputes a quoted price
- Customer requests a memo on a `HOLD`-tagged stone
- Customer requests > 5 concurrent memos
- Customer asks anything about another customer
- Customer claims a stone is mis-graded

Escalate by tagging the Sales Head in the thread (`@sales-head`)
and pausing the conversation until they reply.
